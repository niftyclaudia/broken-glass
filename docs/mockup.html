Broken Glass.

Setting the jello…

Broken Glass is a study of the potluck classic: cubes of cherry, lime, orange and berry-blue jello set in a creamy condensed-milk layer on a graham-cracker crust. Grab it and it wobbles. Slice it and every cut face becomes a new mosaic. Stamp it with a Jiggler cutter and lift the shape right out.

Stained glass you can eat.
Wobbly on purpose.
A potluck favourite, circa ’96.
